Problems will appear after launch. A clear routine for handling them leads to regular improvements and keeps the solution useful over time.

## Checklist

- Review the evaluation results regularly with users and stakeholders.
- Identify problems, gaps and ideas for improvement, and rank them by benefit and effort.
- Revisit the [process map](https://qi.elft.nhs.uk/resource/flow-charts) to see where the workflow or the solution needs to change.
- Test new ideas on a small scale before wider use ([PDSA cycles](https://www.ihi.org/library/model-for-improvement/testing-changes)).
- Treat larger changes as small implementation projects, with a plan, an owner and a follow-up.
- Document each change and share it with users and stakeholders.
- Consider whether the changes also apply to similar initiatives.

## Methods and tools

- **[Process map](https://qi.elft.nhs.uk/resource/flow-charts):** A visual map of a workflow that helps to find problems and chances to improve.
- **[Plan-Do-Study-Act (PDSA) cycles](https://www.ihi.org/library/model-for-improvement/testing-changes):** Short test cycles that allow quick learning and adjustment.
- **Small implementation projects:** A short version of the steps in this guide, used for each larger change.
