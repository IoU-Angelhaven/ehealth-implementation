In healthcare, spreading a solution depends on people and systems as much as on technology. A good plan keeps what is important the same everywhere, allows local flexibility, supports future decision makers and keeps clinical quality and patient experience consistent.

## Checklist

- Decide which parts of the solution must be the same everywhere and which can be adapted locally.
- Describe what is needed to expand, such as infrastructure, support and training, using a structured approach such as the [WHO/ExpandNet scaling-up guide](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241500319).
- Prepare implementation guides and templates that other units can reuse.
- Define how clinical quality and patient experience will be kept consistent at larger scale.
- Give future decision makers a clear plan for spreading the solution, with decision points.

## Methods and tools

- **[Scaling-up strategy (WHO/ExpandNet)](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241500319):** Nine steps for expanding health service innovations that have been tested in pilots.
- **[Benefits management](https://projectdelivery.gov.uk/teal-book/home/part-e-planning-and-control/chapter-19-benefits-management/):** Defines expected benefits and measures, and follows them when the solution is expanded.
- **[Cost-benefit analysis](https://www.atlassian.com/work-management/strategic-planning/cost-benefit-analysis):** Compares the costs of expanding with the expected benefits.
