Evaluate regularly whether the solution has reached its goals and created the wanted effect. The expected results are a set of key measures, a plan for how to collect the data, and a plan for how to measure regularly, fitted into the evaluation routines that already exist in the unit or organization.

## Checklist

- Review the value description and decide what the evaluation must show.
- Define [key measures (KPIs)](https://www.kpi.org/kpi-basics/) for effectiveness (does it work?) and for value (what does it create?).
- Set a starting level and a target for each measure.
- Choose data sources and methods, for example surveys, interviews, patient-reported measures and existing health record data.
- Decide who collects, studies and reports the data, and how often.
- Fit data collection into existing evaluation routines to avoid extra workload.
- Check data quality, privacy and security.
- Review the results regularly and decide whether the goals have been reached.

## Methods and tools

- **[Key performance indicators (KPIs)](https://www.kpi.org/kpi-basics/):** Measures that are linked to the goals of the solution.
- **[Surveys and questionnaires](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/survey-best-practices/):** Collect structured answers from many users, for example on satisfaction or use.
- **[Interviews and focus groups](https://education.nsw.gov.au/teaching-and-learning/professional-learning/pl-resources/evaluation-resource-hub/collecting-data/interviews-and-focus-groups):** Collect in-depth views and experiences from users and stakeholders.
- **[Patient-reported outcome measures (PROMs)](https://www.ahrq.gov/cahps/about-cahps/patient-experience/prems-proms/index.html):** Questionnaires where patients report their own health, symptoms and function.
- **[Clinical audit and chart review](https://www.rcpath.org/profession/patient-safety-and-quality-improvement/conducting-a-clinical-audit.html):** Check records and practice against agreed standards, using clinical and administrative data.
- **[RE-AIM framework](https://www.re-aim.org/):** Structures the evaluation around Reach, Effectiveness, Adoption, Implementation and Maintenance.
- **[Mixed methods](https://catalyst.harvard.edu/community-engagement/mmr/):** Combine numbers and personal experiences to get a fuller picture.
