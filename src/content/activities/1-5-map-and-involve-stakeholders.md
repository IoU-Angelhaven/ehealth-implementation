Find out who they are, what role they have, what interests and concerns they have, and how they should be involved. This clarifies responsibilities and supports cooperation. It also shows possible champions and the groups that need extra support or information.

## Checklist

- List all people, teams and organizations that are affected by the project or can influence it.
- Sort them by their level of influence and their level of interest.
- Identify possible champions: people who can support and speak for the change.
- Identify groups that may resist or need extra information and support.
- For each group, define what they need to know and how and when to involve them.
- Decide who should be informed, who should be asked for input and who should take an active part.
- Plan communication or activities for the key groups.
- Update the map at important points in the project.

## Methods and tools

- **[Stakeholder analysis](https://asana.com/resources/project-stakeholder):** Sorts stakeholders on a grid by influence and interest.
- **[RACI matrix](https://www.atlassian.com/work-management/project-management/raci-chart):** Shows who is Responsible, Accountable, Consulted and Informed.
- **[Communication plan](https://www.smartsheet.com/content/project-communications-plan):** Describes messages, audiences, channels, timing and who is responsible.
- **[Champions](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13012-020-01024-9):** Respected colleagues who support the change in their own teams.
