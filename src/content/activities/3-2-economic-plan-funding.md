Poor financial planning often leads to delays, lower quality or a project that is stopped. A clear plan avoids funding gaps during implementation and shows funders and leaders why the investment is worthwhile.

## Checklist

- List all costs. Separate one-time implementation costs from running costs such as staff, maintenance, support and improvement.
- Estimate staff and project costs for each phase.
- Identify possible funding sources and check how realistic each one is.
- Compare costs and benefits ([cost-benefit analysis](https://www.atlassian.com/work-management/strategic-planning/cost-benefit-analysis)). Include benefits that are hard to measure, such as patient safety and staff satisfaction.
- Add a reserve for unexpected needs, typically 10–20% of the project cost.
- Assess the financial risks and test best-case, likely and worst-case outcomes ([scenario analysis](https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/financial-modeling/scenario-analysis/)).
- Prepare an investment case ([business case](https://www.atlassian.com/work-management/project-management/business-case)) for leadership and obtain formal budget approval.
- Set up regular tracking of spending against budget and of the benefits achieved.

## Methods and tools

- **[Cost-benefit analysis](https://www.atlassian.com/work-management/strategic-planning/cost-benefit-analysis):** Compare the costs of implementation with the expected benefits, in money where possible.
- **[Return on investment (ROI)](https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/accounting/return-on-investment-roi/):** Shows the financial return compared with the investment, for example as a percentage or a payback time.
- **[Scenario analysis](https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/financial-modeling/scenario-analysis/):** Describes best-case, likely and worst-case financial outcomes to support decisions.
- **[Business case](https://www.atlassian.com/work-management/project-management/business-case):** A document that explains costs, benefits and risks, and why the investment is justified.
