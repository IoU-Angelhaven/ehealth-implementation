Working together with stakeholders also builds shared ownership and a clearer direction for the next steps.

## Checklist

- Invite a mix of participants: clinical staff, administrative staff, patients, IT and managers.
- Prepare a short summary of the current situation and its pain points, using the results from Phase 1.
- Book idea sessions with protected time.
- Write down all ideas during the session without filtering them.
- Sort and group the ideas after the session.
- Assess the best ideas for how much they help and how easy they are to carry out.
- Present the chosen ideas to a wider group and ask for feedback.
- Agree which ideas to develop into early versions.

## Methods and tools

- **[Brainstorming](https://hr.mit.edu/learning-topics/meetings/articles/brainstorming):** A group session where many ideas are collected quickly, without criticism.
- **[“How might we…?” questions](https://www.designkit.org/methods/how-might-we.html):** Open questions that start a creative discussion.
- **[Scenarios and role play](https://servicedesigntools.org/tools/role-playing):** Act out a situation to understand how an idea would work in practice.
- **[Co-creation](https://ijic.org/articles/10.5334/ijic.5573):** Staff, patients and others develop ideas together.
- **[Impact-effort matrix](https://www.learnleansigma.com/guides/impact-effort-matrix/):** A simple grid that sorts ideas by how much they help and how much work they need.
