A clear value description aligns the initiative with the organization’s priorities, strengthens engagement among clinicians and stakeholders, supports funding and improves decisions about wider rollout.

## Checklist

- Describe why the initiative matters and who benefits: patients, staff and the organization.
- Identify the most valuable results of the pilot and describe them in clear terms ([value proposition canvas](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/the-value-proposition-canvas)).
- Support the benefits with data, for example [key performance indicators (KPIs)](https://www.kpi.org/kpi-basics/).
- Link the benefits to the strategic priorities of the organization.
- Describe how the benefits will be delivered and measured over time.
- Adapt the message to each audience, especially management ([stakeholder analysis](https://asana.com/resources/project-stakeholder)).
- Prepare clear presentations and visuals ([communication plan](https://www.smartsheet.com/content/project-communications-plan)).

## Methods and tools

- **[Value proposition canvas](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/the-value-proposition-canvas):** Matches what users need with what the solution offers.
- **[Key performance indicators (KPIs)](https://www.kpi.org/kpi-basics/):** Measures that show progress and results with data.
- **[Benefits management](https://projectdelivery.gov.uk/teal-book/home/part-e-planning-and-control/chapter-19-benefits-management/):** Identifies and describes benefits and follows them over time.
- **[Stakeholder analysis](https://asana.com/resources/project-stakeholder):** Shows who needs which message.
- **[Communication plan](https://www.smartsheet.com/content/project-communications-plan):** Defines messages, audiences, channels and timing.
