This gives the implementation team a record of what you have learned and a common reference for stakeholders. It supports training and change management, and shows the value of a wider rollout.

## Checklist

- Agree what the future model covers: which processes, departments and patient groups.
- Map the future process in the same way as the current process.
- Mark where technology and connections between systems are needed.
- Draw journey maps that show the wanted experience for patients and staff.
- Document decision points and who to contact when problems arise.
- Describe the specific benefits that the new way of working creates.
- Write a short description in plain language for readers without technical background.
- Ask clinical experts to check that it is correct.

## Methods and tools

- **[Process map](https://qi.elft.nhs.uk/resource/flow-charts) and [swimlane diagram](https://lucid.co/diagram/swimlane/tutorial):** Show the steps and who does what.
- **[Service blueprint](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/service-blueprints-definition/):** Shows what the patient sees and what happens behind the scenes.
- **[Patient journey map](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/journey-mapping-101/):** Shows the wanted patient experience from start to end.
- **[Data flow diagram](https://lucid.co/diagram/dfd/tutorial):** Shows how information moves between people and systems.
