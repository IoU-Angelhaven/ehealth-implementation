## Checklist

- Decide what to compare: a process, a result, a technology or a way of implementing.
- Find organizations or projects to compare with, for example with similar size, setting or patient groups.
- Collect information about similar implementations: what was done, how, and with what results.
- Look for good examples and for warning examples.
- Write down the main lessons, the results and factors that may affect whether it works in your setting.
- Talk to or visit colleagues in other organizations or networks who faced similar challenges.
- Use what you learn to sharpen your goals and decisions.

## Methods and tools

- **[Literature review](https://guides.lib.uoguelph.ca/LiteratureReview):** A search for and summary of reports, articles and official publications.
- **[Interviews with experts](https://education.nsw.gov.au/teaching-and-learning/professional-learning/pl-resources/evaluation-resource-hub/collecting-data/interviews-and-focus-groups):** Talk to people who have led similar implementations elsewhere.
- **[Networks and communities of practice](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/ehealth):** For example EU eHealth initiatives, where organizations share experience.
