Clarify who will work on the project continuously and who can help when needed.

## Checklist

- Decide who are core team members (with dedicated time) and who are advisers (asked when needed).
- Appoint a project lead who has the authority to make decisions.
- Agree each person’s time and responsibilities.
- Plan regular team meetings and choose how the team will communicate.
- Hold a kick-off meeting so that the team starts together.

## Methods and tools

- **[Team charter](https://www.atlassian.com/work-management/project-collaboration/team-charter):** A short agreement about the team’s purpose, roles, ways of working and how decisions are made.
- **[Kick-off meeting](https://thedigitalprojectmanager.com/project-management/how-to-run-a-great-project-kick-off-meeting/):** A first meeting where the team agrees on goals, roles and next steps.
