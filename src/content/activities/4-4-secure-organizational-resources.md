It turns digital change from something extra into a core part of the organization’s work. The expected results are funding and staffing that continue after the pilot, protected time for staff, clear ownership and executive support.

## Checklist

- Build a [business case](https://www.atlassian.com/work-management/project-management/business-case) that shows why the solution is essential for patient care and not optional.
- Calculate the benefits in terms of quality, safety, time and cost ([cost-benefit analysis](https://www.atlassian.com/work-management/strategic-planning/cost-benefit-analysis)).
- Secure budget and funding decisions that continue beyond the pilot period.
- Secure staffing for running and supporting the solution ([resource planning](https://www.atlassian.com/work-management/project-management/resource-planning)).
- Protect time for clinicians and staff to learn and use the new way of working.
- Set up support, such as a help desk, super-users and technical support.
- Agree on ownership, accountability and executive support in writing.
- Reduce the dependence on short-term project funding and on individual champions.

## Methods and tools

- **[Business case](https://www.atlassian.com/work-management/project-management/business-case):** A document that explains the costs, benefits and risks, and why the investment is justified.
- **[Cost-benefit analysis](https://www.atlassian.com/work-management/strategic-planning/cost-benefit-analysis):** Compares costs and benefits to support budget and funding decisions.
- **[Resource planning](https://www.atlassian.com/work-management/project-management/resource-planning):** Defines the roles, the number of people and the time needed.
- **[Champions and super-users](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13012-020-01024-9):** Part of the support, but not a replacement for dedicated resources.
