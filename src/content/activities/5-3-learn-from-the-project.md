The result is a document or report for stakeholders. It highlights the achievements of the project and the areas to improve in future implementation projects.

## Checklist

- Plan a [post-implementation review](https://www.mindtools.com/a192l7e/post-implementation-reviews/) when the solution has been in use for some time.
- Invite the work group, users and other stakeholders.
- Hold a [retrospective workshop](https://community.atlassian.com/forums/Teamwork-Lab-articles/A-facilitator-s-guide-to-running-a-Retrospective/ba-p/1505583) to look at what worked, what did not and why.
- Review the work group, the organization, the process and the activities that were carried out.
- Write a short report with achievements, challenges and recommendations.
- Share the report with stakeholders and leaders.
- Store the report where future projects can find and use it.

## Methods and tools

- **[Retrospective workshop](https://community.atlassian.com/forums/Teamwork-Lab-articles/A-facilitator-s-guide-to-running-a-Retrospective/ba-p/1505583):** A facilitated meeting where the team reflects on what went well, what did not and what to change.
- **[Post-implementation review](https://www.mindtools.com/a192l7e/post-implementation-reviews/):** A review after the project to check whether the goals were met and how well the project was run.
