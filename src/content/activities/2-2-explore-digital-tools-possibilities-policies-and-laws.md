## Checklist

- List the digital tools and systems that already exist in your organization.
- Check how data protection rules, such as GDPR and national patient data laws, affect your project.
- If needed, learn about organizational policies that matter, such as purchasing, IT management and change procedures.
- Check clinical guidelines and legal requirements for your specialty.
- Find out which funding and purchasing routes are available.
- Look at similar solutions in comparable organizations.
- Book meetings with IT leaders, compliance staff and purchasing staff.

## Methods and tools

- **[EU data protection rules (GDPR)](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/data-protection-explained_en):** The European Commission’s overview of the rules for handling personal data.
- **[Interviews](https://education.nsw.gov.au/teaching-and-learning/professional-learning/pl-resources/evaluation-resource-hub/collecting-data/interviews-and-focus-groups):** Talk to IT leaders, compliance officers and data protection officers.
- **[Gap analysis](https://www.learnleansigma.com/guides/gap-analysis/):** Compare what you have today with what you need, and write down the differences.
