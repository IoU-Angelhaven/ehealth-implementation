Without this knowledge, even a well-designed solution can miss real needs.

## Checklist

- Define the main user groups, for example patients, nurses, doctors and administrative staff.
- Choose methods to learn about the needs and situation of each group.
- Carry out interviews, observations or surveys, and write down the findings in a structured way.
- Observe users in their real work environment when possible.
- Look for repeated themes, problems, needs and workarounds.
- Share and check the findings with the team and stakeholders.
- Use the findings to describe the requirements and the problem.

## Methods and tools

- **[Interviews and focus groups](https://education.nsw.gov.au/teaching-and-learning/professional-learning/pl-resources/evaluation-resource-hub/collecting-data/interviews-and-focus-groups):** Talk to one person or a small group to learn about their experiences and needs.
- **[Observation at the workplace (field study)](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/field-studies/):** Watch people work in their normal setting.
- **[Surveys and questionnaires](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/survey-best-practices/):** Short questionnaires to many people.
- **[Diary study](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/diary-studies/):** Users note their experiences over time.
- **[Personas](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/persona/):** Short descriptions of typical users, based on research.
- **[Affinity diagram](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/affinity-diagram/):** Groups notes from interviews and observations into themes.
