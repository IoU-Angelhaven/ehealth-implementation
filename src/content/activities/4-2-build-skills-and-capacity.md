The expected results are higher digital skills among staff and support for continuous learning. Learning should be part of everyday clinical work, not something added on top of it.

## Checklist

- Assess the digital skills of staff (the [European digital competence framework](https://joint-research-centre.ec.europa.eu/projects-and-activities/education-and-training/digital-transformation-education/digital-competence-framework-digcomp/digcomp-30_en) can be used as a reference).
- Compare the skills needed with the current skills ([skills gap analysis](https://www.aihr.com/blog/skills-gap-analysis/)).
- Build learning into daily work, for example through short sessions, practice on the job and protected learning time.
- Offer [coaching and mentoring](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/money-business/workplace-learning-coaching-and-mentoring/content-section-3) by experienced colleagues and super-users.
- Adjust roles and job plans so that time for learning and new tasks is included.
- Plan refresher training and an introduction for new staff.
- Follow how skills develop, and adjust the support when needed.

## Methods and tools

- **[Digital competence framework (DigComp)](https://joint-research-centre.ec.europa.eu/projects-and-activities/education-and-training/digital-transformation-education/digital-competence-framework-digcomp/digcomp-30_en):** A European reference for describing and assessing digital skills.
- **[Skills gap analysis](https://www.aihr.com/blog/skills-gap-analysis/):** Compares the skills needed with the skills available to show training needs.
- **[Coaching and mentoring](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/money-business/workplace-learning-coaching-and-mentoring/content-section-3):** Gives staff personal support from a coach or an experienced colleague.
- **[Champions and super-users](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13012-020-01024-9):** Colleagues who give practical support and encourage use in their own teams.
