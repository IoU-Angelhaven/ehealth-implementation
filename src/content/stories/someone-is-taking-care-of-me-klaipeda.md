## The starting point

Patients followed exercise programs on a digital platform. Contact with patients was especially important in the first two weeks.

## What we noticed

The platform had very few tools for feedback. Patients could only send a smiley. That was not enough to keep in touch when patients needed it most.

## What we tried

We sent text messages directly to patients' phones. We also updated each patient's exercise program frequently.

## What we learned

Frequent updates built trust and motivation. Patients felt that "someone is taking care of me". [Check the wording of the quote with the site. Did it come from patients?]

## Our tip for others

If the technology cannot keep the contact with patients, find another way. Personal contact matters most at the start.
