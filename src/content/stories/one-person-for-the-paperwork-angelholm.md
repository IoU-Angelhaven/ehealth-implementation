## The starting point

Our physiotherapists delivered digital rehabilitation to patients during cancer treatment. At the same time, they handled the study tasks: consent, questionnaires and functional testing.

## What we noticed

The physiotherapists were overloaded. It was hard to combine study administration with normal clinical work.

## What we tried

We made one person responsible for all study-related tasks. [Who took on the role, and how was it set up?]

## What we learned

The other physiotherapists could focus on delivering rehabilitation. [What difference did this make for staff and patients?]

## Our tip for others

Keep administration and care delivery apart when you can. A clear owner for each task protects the time for patients.
