## The starting point

Patients did their exercises at home with a sensor that tracked their movements. [How was the sensor meant to be used?]

## What we noticed

Some patients could not comfortably hold the sensor during their exercises. One example was arm exercises after breast cancer.

## What we tried

We designed custom sensor holders, taped in place, so patients did not need to hold the sensor. [How did you develop them? Did you try several versions?]

## What we learned

Small, practical changes can decide whether a patient can use the technology at all. [What did patients say about the holders?]

## Our tip for others

Watch patients use the technology in real life. The solution to a problem can be simple and cheap.
