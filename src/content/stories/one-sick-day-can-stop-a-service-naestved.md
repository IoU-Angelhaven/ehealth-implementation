## The starting point

A small team of physiotherapists ran digital physiotherapy locally. [How many people were involved at the start?]

## What we noticed

The team was so small that one person leaving or going on sick leave could put the whole local service at risk.

## What we tried

We involved more physiotherapists as participants in the project. [How did you bring them in and train them?]

## What we learned

A service that depends on a few people is fragile. Sharing knowledge across more staff makes it more robust. [Did this change how the service ran day to day?]

## Our tip for others

Do not build a new service around one or two key people. Involve more colleagues early, before you need them.
