## The starting point

We asked several clinical teams at the hospital to refer cancer patients to digital physiotherapy.

## What we noticed

Engagement varied a lot between teams. The oncology and radiation team was fully on board and referred everyone. The breast cancer team referred almost no one, even when asked. The prostate cancer team often forgot to refer.

## What we tried

We adapted our approach to each team. For the breast cancer team, a member of the project team approached all patients directly. For the prostate cancer team, we put up physical reminder notes for the doctors.

## What we learned

One approach does not fit every team. [What did you learn about why the teams responded so differently?]

## Our tip for others

Find out how each team works, and adapt your approach to them rather than asking everyone the same way.
