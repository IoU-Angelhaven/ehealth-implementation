## The starting point

We offered digital physiotherapy to patients with several types of cancer. Awareness was low that exercise during cancer treatment is possible and beneficial.

## What we noticed

Doctors are not located together with the physiotherapists. They had neither the time nor the detailed knowledge to identify patients who could take part.

## What we tried

We hired dedicated administrative staff to find and recruit patients. [How did they work with the doctors?]
