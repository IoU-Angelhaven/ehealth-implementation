## The starting point

We depended on physicians to refer patients to digital physiotherapy. At first, we asked them to refer patients in general terms.

## What we noticed

Many referring physicians forgot or did not refer. Whether a physician was engaged depended very much on the individual person.

## What we tried

We changed from passive requests to a much more direct approach. We named specific patients and handed over flyers to physicians in person.

## What we learned

[What happened to the number of referrals after the change?] Referrals depend on people, and personal contact works better than general requests.

## Our tip for others

Do not wait for referrals to come to you. Go to the people who refer, and make it easy for them.
