## The starting point

We offered digital physiotherapy to patients during cancer treatment. [How were patients informed about the study and the digital intervention before the change?]

## What we noticed

[What showed us that patients needed an introduction before the first appointment?]

## What we tried

We added a phone call before the first appointment. In the call, we introduced the study and explained what it means to take part in a digital intervention. At first, the calls felt awkward. They became easier with practice.

## What we learned

The phone call became a lasting improvement to our process, independent of the study itself. [What did the call give patients and staff?]

## Our tip for others

Give a new routine time. What feels awkward the first few times can become a natural part of the work.
