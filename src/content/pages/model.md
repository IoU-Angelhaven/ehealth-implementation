Each activity has four parts: a short introduction, a checklist, methods and tools with links to pages that explain them, and examples of prompts for an AI assistant. The phases are not strictly one after the other. You can go back to an earlier phase when you learn something new.

## Words used in this guide

- **Solution:** The new service, tool or way of working that you want to introduce.
- **Pilot:** A small test of the solution in real daily work, before it is used more widely.
- **Stakeholders:** The people and groups who are affected by the work or can influence it.
- **Champions and super-users:** Colleagues who support the change and help their peers to use the solution.
- **Early version (prototype):** A simple first version of an idea, such as a drawing or a paper model, that is used for testing.

## Using the AI prompt examples

- Replace the text in [square brackets] with your own information.
- When a prompt says “Attach”, add the named documents to your conversation with the AI assistant. The answers are much better when the AI has your own documents as background.
- Do not attach documents that contain patient information or other personal data. Remove names and other details that identify a person first, and follow your organization’s rules for using AI.
- Always check the answers. AI can make mistakes, especially about laws, rules and examples from other organizations.
