## Automatic translation

The website is written in English. The language menu at the top of each page lets you read a page machine-translated into Swedish, Danish, German, Polish or Lithuanian. The translation is made by Google Translate: when you choose a language, the page opens on Google's translation service (an address ending in translate.goog), and Google's privacy policy applies there. Nothing is sent to Google unless you choose a language. Machine translations may contain errors; the English text is the original.
