icon: cycle
summary: "This phase makes sure that a new way of working lasts after the pilot has ended. In healthcare, a change that works in the short term is not enough. It must stay effective, safe and affordable over time, even when demands and resources change."
keyMessage: |-
  Sustainable practice protects quality of care, staff well-being and efficiency. It also builds resilience and a culture of innovation and development that continues over time.

  The phase builds on the results of Phase 3, such as the pilot results, the approved financial plan and engaged stakeholders. It has six activities: long-term planning, building skills, planning for spreading the solution to more units, securing organizational resources, describing the value, and setting up leadership and decision-making. Together, they turn a pilot into a permanent part of the organization’s work.
