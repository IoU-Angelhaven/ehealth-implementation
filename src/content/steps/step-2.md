title: "Solution Design"
icon: lightbulb
summary: "In this phase you start to explore the conditions for solutions and the possible solutions. Ideas can be tested early, before they have become finished solutions. Later they can be tried in real work (this is called a pilot and is part of the next phase)."
---

This phase must be flexible. You do not go through it only once. It can be a space for generating ideas, discussing and testing throughout the whole implementation. Keep an open attitude, and let the team, management, patients and other stakeholders react to the ideas. This gives useful knowledge about which ideas are worth more time and resources.
